Why Facilities Automate

Space, Accuracy, and Labor Don't Scale on Their Own

Manual storage and picking hit a ceiling fast—more volume just means more racking, more travel time, and more people. Automation changes that math.

We partner with BoxLogix to plan and deploy AS/RS, shuttle systems, and robotics that pack more inventory into the same footprint, pick with far less error, and take repetitive travel and lifting off your team. Their Logix warehouse control system ties it all together, so equipment, software, and your existing operation move as one coordinated system instead of a collection of standalone machines.

What We Evaluate

  • Throughput targets—lines per hour, order profile, and peak volume
  • SKU mix, storage density needs, and available building height
  • How automation integrates with your WMS, ERP, and existing equipment
  • Scalability—room to add modules or throughput as demand grows
  • Facility fit, including power, floor loading, and clearances
  • Uptime requirements, service coverage, and spare parts access
Automation Systems

High-Density, High-Speed Storage—Built Around Your Operation

Throughput needs, SKU velocity, and building height look different at every facility. We compare shuttle, crane, and robotic platforms to build an automation strategy around how your operation actually runs.

Unit-Load & Mini-Load AS/RS

Cranes and shuttles store and retrieve pallets or totes with pinpoint accuracy, packing far more inventory into the same footprint than manual racking.

Best fit: high-volume, high-density storage

Shuttle Systems

Single-level, multi-level, and multi-deep shuttles move totes and cartons through dense storage lanes, built to scale with added shuttles or levels.

Best fit: fast-moving SKUs needing rapid cycle times

Vertical Lift Modules

Enclosed vertical storage delivers parts and small items straight to an operator, trading aisle space for height and cutting search time to seconds.

Best fit: small parts, tight footprints, secure storage

Goods-to-Person Picking

Inventory comes to the operator instead of the other way around, cutting travel time and letting one picker work multiple orders at once.

Best fit: high-SKU e-commerce and each-picking operations

Robotic Storage & Retrieval

Autonomous mobile robots and robotic cranes handle putaway, replenishment, and retrieval, working alongside staff without a full facility overhaul.

Best fit: phased automation and labor-constrained operations

Integrated Buffer & Transport

Conveyor and buffer zones link automated storage to packing, sortation, and shipping, keeping product flowing between manual and automated areas.

Best fit: connecting automation into an existing facility

Logix Warehouse Control System

The software layer that orchestrates every AS/RS, shuttle, and robot in the system—syncing them with your WMS and giving you one view of performance.

Best fit: any automated system needing central control

Hybrid Automation Retrofits

Automation layered into an existing rack, mezzanine, and conveyor footprint—built to expand in phases instead of requiring a ground-up rebuild.

Best fit: brownfield facilities automating in stages
