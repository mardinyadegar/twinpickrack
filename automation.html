Why Facilities Automate

Space, Accuracy, and Labor Don't Scale on Their Own

Manual storage and picking hit a ceiling fast—more volume just means more racking, more travel time, and more people. Automation changes that math.

Twin Pick Rack and BoxLogix, our Automation Partner, plan and deploy AS/RS, shuttle systems, and robotics that pack more inventory into the same footprint, pick with far less error, and take repetitive travel and lifting off your team. Their Logix warehouse control system ties it all together, so equipment, software, and your existing operation move as one coordinated system instead of a collection of standalone machines.

What We Evaluate

  • Throughput targets—lines per hour, order profile, and peak volume
  • SKU mix, storage density needs, and available building height
  • How automation integrates with your WMS, ERP, and existing equipment
  • Scalability—room to add modules or throughput as demand grows
  • Facility fit, including power, floor loading, and clearances
  • Uptime requirements, service coverage, and spare parts access
