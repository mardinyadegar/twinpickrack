Storage Systems

Why Choose Industrial Shelving Systems

Industrial shelving covers the small-item end of storage that pallet rack isn't built for—bins, cartons, parts, and piece-pick inventory. Through WPSS, we spec boltless RiveTier shelving for fast, tool-free assembly and heavy-duty Deluxe shelving engineered to seismic code for higher-load applications.

Beyond standard bin shelving, WPSS also builds specialty configurations—put walls for batch and each-picking, automotive parts storage, and AMR-ready racking sized and reinforced for robotic fulfillment systems. It's the layer that usually sits above or alongside pallet rack in a fully built-out warehouse.

Where It Fits Best

Is Industrial Shelving Systems Right for Your Facility?

Best fit: Small-item storage, order picking, and automation-ready facilities.

Talk with us about your layout, throughput, and equipment before locking in a system—we'll help confirm whether this is the right call or whether another approach fits better.
