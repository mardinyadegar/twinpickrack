The Porvene Series

One Door Line, Seven Ways to Close an Opening

Porvene has built commercial doors since 1968, starting with the continuous steel sheet door and adding heavy-duty rolling steel slat doors in 1990.

The lineup below runs from economical sheet and slat doors to insulated and high-cycle systems. We help you match the series, gauge, and options to each opening, then handle sourcing and installation alongside the rest of your access plan.

Series 100

Commercial Sectional Door

Best fit: Loading docks and commercial openings that need a tight weather seal and flexible track options.

The Model 124 family (124, 124-VB, 124-SB) uses 2″-thick, 24-gauge ribbed galvanized sections with tongue-and-groove joints. Springs are engineered for each door's weight, size, and track layout.

Key Features

  • Single-layer, vinyl-backed, or steel-backed insulated models
  • Torsion springs rated for 10,000 cycles, with 25,000–100,000 cycle upgrades
  • Standard, high-lift, full vertical, and low-headroom track
  • Bottom weather seal and powder-coated color options
Series 200

The Original Sheet Door

Best fit: Self-storage, mini-warehouses, and secure openings where low maintenance matters most.

A continuous galvanized steel sheet curtain rolls up into a compact drum that houses the counterbalance. Commercial, industrial, insulated, and windlocked models are available.

Key Features

  • Greaseless nylon bearings that outlast metal without upkeep
  • Lock-seamed sections that can be swapped out if damaged
  • Tamper-resistant slide bolt that takes two padlocks
  • Options for vision lites, vents, wicket doors, and electric operators
Series 300

The Commercial Slat Door

Best fit: Budget-conscious commercial and retail openings that still need a secure steel curtain.

An economy rolling slat door in 24 or 22 gauge galvanized steel, with curved or flat slats finished in a baked epoxy primer and polyester topcoat.

Key Features

  • Two slide bolt locks standard on every door
  • Self-aligning sealed ball bearings for smooth rotation
  • Springs sized on Porvene's in-house door dynamometer
  • Over 150 powder-coat colors available
Series 400

The Rolling Steel Service Door

Best fit: High-use industrial, warehouse, and service openings that take daily abuse.

A heavy-duty slat curtain in 24, 22, 20, or 18 gauge steel runs in structural steel angle guides and rides on a continuous 6″ pipe barrel built to limit deflection across wide openings.

Key Features

  • Flat or curved slats (18 gauge in curved only)
  • Structural steel bottom bar with rubber astragal
  • #50 roller chain hoist; ready for an electric operator
  • Inside and outside door stops for added safety
Series 500

The Quiet Glide Rolling Grille

Best fit: Storefronts, parking garages, and openings that need security without blocking airflow or visibility.

Anodized aluminum tubes over steel rods form an open grille curtain, guided by extruded aluminum tracks with polyethylene wear strips for quiet operation.

Key Features

  • Clear, light bronze, dark bronze, black, or gold anodized finish
  • Nylon-blocked bottom bar for smooth, quiet travel
  • Push-up operation on grilles up to 100 sq ft
  • Electric operators, hoods, and security patterns available
Series 600

Therm-Master Insulated Door

Best fit: Climate-controlled, conditioned, and noise-sensitive spaces.

A polyisocyanurate insulated core sits between a 22 gauge front slat and a 24 gauge back slat, with vinyl jamb weatherseal and a baffled hood to keep the elements out.

Key Features

  • R-5.4 insulation value (U-factor 0.185)
  • STC-23 sound transmission rating
  • Vision lites, pass doors, and high-cycle operation options
  • Mohegan White standard, with over 150 powder-coat colors
Accelerated Action System

Rapid-Opening Rolling Steel & Sectional Doors

Best fit: Fire stations, emergency vehicle bays, and busy forklift openings where every second counts.

Developed with Manaras, the system opens doors at 2 feet per second—a 14-foot door in about 7 seconds, roughly twice as fast as a conventional setup—and can be retrofitted to existing openings without reconstruction.

Key Features

  • Works with Porvene sectional, rolling steel, insulated, and grille models
  • Variable frequency drive with soft start and soft stop
  • Integrates with firehouse and standard commercial controls
  • Lower cost than a four-fold high-speed door system
