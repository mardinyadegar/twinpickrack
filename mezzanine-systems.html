Your Next Square Foot Is Already Overhead

Most facilities have unused vertical space sitting in plain sight.

We look at your available height, workflow, structural requirements, safety considerations, and long-term growth plans before recommending a mezzanine or platform. Working with manufacturers like FCP Mezzanine keeps the resulting environment flexible enough to grow with the business.

What We Evaluate

  • Ceiling height, column spacing, and other building constraints
  • Load capacity needed for people, equipment, inventory, and operations
  • Workflow needs—storage, picking, production, or office space
  • Stairways, guardrails, gates, fall protection, and code compliance
  • How it connects with conveyors, pallet handling, and existing equipment
  • Room to expand—modular design and long-term flexibility
Platform Options

What to Build Depends on What You Need Up There

Storage, production space, offices, or elevated picking—whatever you're adding, the right mezzanine solution starts with understanding how your operation runs today.

01

Structural Mezzanines

An elevated deck adds usable work and storage area over your existing floor plan, no building expansion required.

Best fit: growing operations tight on floor space
02

Equipment Platforms

Purpose-built to carry machinery, automation systems, conveyors, and production equipment overhead, freeing up the floor space underneath.

Best fit: manufacturing and automation installs
03

Pick Modules

Mezzanines, shelving, conveyors, and workstations combine into one multi-level picking system built for fast, accurate order fulfillment.

Best fit: distribution and e-commerce fulfillment centers
04

Catwalk Systems

Elevated walkways give crews safe, direct access to equipment, conveyors, storage, and maintenance points overhead.

Best fit: multi-level facilities needing maintenance access
05

Stair Systems & Safety Railings

OSHA-compliant stairs, guardrails, handrails, and gates keep movement between levels safe and code-compliant.

Best fit: any facility with elevated work areas
06

Modular Office Systems

Build climate-controlled offices, conference rooms, QC labs, or break rooms above the warehouse floor instead of carving into it.

Best fit: adding office space inside an existing building
07

Integrated Conveyor & Mezzanine Systems

Pairing elevated conveyors with mezzanine decking keeps product moving between levels without breaking up the workflow.

Best fit: high-throughput fulfillment operations
08

Custom Warehouse Infrastructure

Every operation is different, so we design complete infrastructure—mezzanines, conveyors, pallet rack, safety systems, and automation—as one cohesive plan built around yours.

Best fit: facilities planning long-term growth
