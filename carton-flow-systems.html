Carton Flow Systems

SpanTrack Lane steel roller tracks drop straight into your pallet rack, rolling cartons forward to the pick face while restocking happens from behind.

Request a Rack Assessment ← All Storage Systems
SpanTrack Lane by UNEX

Why Choose Carton Flow Systems

Our carton flow systems are built around UNEX SpanTrack Lane, an engineered-to-order steel roller track that sets directly onto the beams of new or existing pallet rack. There are no shelf decks to add and no mid-span supports to install—cartons load at the back, roll down a gentle pitch, and stop at a pick face that refills itself.

Instead of balancing each carton on a few rows of plastic wheels, SpanTrack Lane carries it on full-width steel rollers, giving roughly three times the product contact of wheel rail. That extra support keeps soft-bottomed, oversized, and heavier cases from snagging partway down the lane, so product keeps moving and pick rates hold steady even in the busiest fulfillment zones and assembly lines.

Picking Styles

Set Up for Full Cases or Single Items

Full Case Picking

Low-profile SpanTrack lanes turn ordinary pallet rack positions into first-in, first-out case lanes. Pickers pull whole cartons from the front while replenishment loads from the aisle behind, so the two jobs never compete for the same space. UNEX reports throughput gains of up to 150% over picking straight from pallets.

Each Picking

Add adjustable pick trays or a knuckled front end and open cases tip toward the picker, putting individual items in plain view and within easy reach. Less bending and stretching means faster, more accurate piece picks and a more comfortable shift for the people doing them.

Where It Fits Best

Is Carton Flow Systems Right for Your Facility?

Best fit: High-volume case and piece picking from new or existing pallet rack.

SpanTrack Lane earns its place anywhere cartons need to reach a picker quickly: e-commerce and order fulfillment centers, lineside racks and supermarkets in manufacturing plants, retail backrooms, and distribution warehouses that want more SKUs in the pick path without a full re-rack. Because the track is rated for freezer and cooler duty below zero, it also suits cold storage and food operations where first-in, first-out rotation protects shelf life.

We'll review your SKU mix, case sizes, and existing rack before recommending lane widths, duty rating, and end treatments—and we'll tell you if another approach fits better.

Key Advantages

  • Drops into new or existing pallet rack—no decks or extra supports
  • Steel rollers give about 300% more carton contact than plastic wheels
  • Light, standard, and heavy-duty tracks built to the inch
  • Freezer and cooler rated to below zero
  • Backed by a 7-year manufacturer warranty
